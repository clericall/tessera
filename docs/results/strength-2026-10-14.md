## Honest caveats (this is a smoke, not a conclusive SPRT)
- Fast TC + 16 games; **not** a SPRT to significance. The real protocol (per project rules) is
  **10+0.1 then 60+0.6, many more games, SPRT with error bars vs identical limits** — pending.
- Fairness notes: Stockfish got `Threads=1`/`Hash=16`; Tessera ignores `setoption` (warnings) so it
  is single-threaded by construction with its own ~10 MB fixed TT. For a canonical match, Tessera
  should honor `Threads`/`Hash`; also clear its TT on `ucinewgame` (currently persists — minor).
- Expected outcome: material-only 10x10 engine vs a mature NNUE engine at these limits loses hard.
  This match validates the harness (fastchess + tier-matched SF + our UCI) so future Elo deltas
  (after bitboards, PST, NNUE, tuning) are measurable and honest.

## Reproduce
```
fastchess -engine cmd=compiler\samples\uci.exe name=Tessera `
  -engine cmd=<sf-universal.exe> name=Stockfish `
  -each tc=5+0.05 option.Threads=1 option.Hash=16 `
  -openings file=engine\book.epd format=epd order=random -games 8 -concurrency 1
```
